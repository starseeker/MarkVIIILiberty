Caterpillar Assembly

The driving sprockets are placed on the tank, fol-
lowed by the track shoes, consisting of two tracks of
78 shoes each. The tracks are assembled to the hull
as follows: Four sections comprise the complete as-
sembly; two sections are laid upon the floor, the
proper length of the hull. The hull is then raised
and set upon these sections. A crane cable is then
hooked to one end of another section and raised high
enough so that the link can be coupled to the section
which is on the floor. It is then draivn along the top
of the tank, at the same time lowering the track to the
hull. The tracks having been adjusted to proper ten-
sion on the hull by the track-adjusting screws, the
tank is ready for road test.
