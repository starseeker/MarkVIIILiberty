A quantity of the armor plate was received without
heat treatment, and it was necessary that this armor
plate be heat-treated before it could be used in the
assembly of tanks. As this was practically the first
time any work of this nature had been attempted at
this arsenal; it was necessary to do a certain amount
of experimenting to produce satisfactory results.
