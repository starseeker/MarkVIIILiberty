# Source readings and editorial notes

The following readings are deliberately preserved, rather than silently corrected.

| Printed page | Reading | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 27 | 30 tanks at one time; approximately 410 square feet; 34 ft. × 12 ft. | Preserve the printed quantities and dimensions. The area is explicitly approximate. |
| 28 | Approximately 3,000 employees; peak 819; virtually 400 per cent turnover | Source values, not the OCR's 38,000 or other noise-contaminated alternatives. |
| 29 | “a reinforced internal gear” | Original grammatical form retained. |
| 29 | “8,000 11-16 inch rivets” | The source prints `11-16`, rather than a slash or stacked fraction. |
| 29 | “The man and officer’s turrets” | “man” is printed clearly; not expanded to “main.” |
| 30 | “Product of Components” | Original heading retained; the next page separately has “Manufacture of Components.” |
| 31 | ⅝-inch Whitworth tap bolts; 20,000 required | Fraction verified against the supplied scan. |
| 31 | “The items ... was probably” | Original grammar retained. |
| 32 | July 1, 1919; June 5, 1920; “286 days” | The printed duration does not equal the calendar interval between the stated dates. No correction is inserted. |
| 32 | January 5, 1920; “150 days”; one tank every 36 hours | Preserve Jordan's production arithmetic as printed. |
| 32 | “semaphone” | The source spells the word **semaphone**, rather than “semaphore,” in the loading-accident paragraph. |
| 32–33 | June 5 ready for road test; June 10 last completion; June 15 promised delivery date | The account distinguishes dates imperfectly; all are retained. The inset letter of June 14 also refers to completion on the 10th. |
| 33 | Workforce averages, arranged in two subcolumns | Reconstructed with separate editable frames to keep each count with its occupation. |

The publication's decorative spacing around punctuation and some small-cap
styling have been regularized. Body line breaks and hyphenated line endings are
retained; the `TRANSCRIPTION.md` file identifies page and column transitions.
Photographic labels were not retyped or embellished. The original source
photographs and their scan limitations remain available for checking those labels.

The scan comparison is a visual review aid, not evidence that an omitted source
margin or unrelated article is part of this reconstruction. The second-page
composite is used exactly once, as page 28.
