MANUFACTURE OF MARK VIII TANKS AT ROCK ISLAND ARSENAL - HARRY B. JORDAN
Errata and ambiguities

Companion to 1920-07_Manufacture_of_Mark_VIII_Tanks.pdf.
References are original article pages 27-33. Source readings remain unchanged.

1. p. 32, duration: July 1, 1919 to June 5, 1920 is stated as 286 days.
   The elapsed calendar interval is 340 days. The article does not establish
   a counting convention that resolves the discrepancy.

2. p. 32, production rate: January 5 to June 5, 1920 is described as 150 days
   and an average of one tank every 36 hours on a 24-hour-day basis. The actual
   elapsed interval is 152 days; 36 hours follows from the article's rounded
   150-day figure and 100 tanks. Treat it as Jordan's stated average.

3. pp. 28, 32-33: p. 32 dates the last tank's completion/readiness for road
   test to June 5; pp. 28 and 33 give June 10 for last completion. The June 14
   congratulatory letter reproduced on p. 27 also says assembly was completed
   on June 10. These may reflect different milestones, but the terminology
   does not fully resolve the difference. June 15 is the promised deadline.

4. p. 29: "The man and officer's turrets" is printed as "man," not "main."
   The rivet dimension is printed "11-16 inch"; the reconstruction retains
   that notation rather than silently changing it to 11/16.

5. p. 30: heading "Product of Components" is retained; p. 31 separately uses
   "Manufacture of Components." p. 32 prints "semaphone" in the accident
   account, rather than "semaphore."

6. Added cover: its source is a bound-volume title page for Volume I,
   July-August 1920 through May-June 1921, not the July-August issue cover.
   The article belongs to No. 1, July-August 1920. Article identification and
   citation on the reconstructed cover are modern editorial additions.
