PRELIMINARY HANDBOOK OF THE MARK VIII TANK
November 15, 1918; reprinted March 6, 1925
Errata and unresolved source readings

One companion file for Preliminary_Handbook_MarkVIII_Tank_Part_1_of_5.pdf
through Preliminary_Handbook_MarkVIII_Tank_Part_5_of_5.pdf.
ALL references below are the handbook's original printed page numbers.

The reconstruction preserves the recorded readings, including apparent source
errors. Proposed interpretations are provisional. Repeated codes, quantities
and dimensions have not been silently reconciled. This is a digest of specific
issues already flagged in the project, not a fresh technical certification.

DIMENSIONS, SPECIFICATIONS AND TECHNICAL DESCRIPTIONS

1. p. 9: ground pressure is printed "975 tons per square foot" with no visible
   decimal before 975. No corrected value is established.

2. p. 32: "5-inch flexible brass tubes (Titeflex)" is the visible reading;
   a missing fractional mark has not been assumed. Similar unexplained plain
   "5" dimensions appear in four petrol-system descriptions on p. 213.

3. p. 45: brake horsepower says only "At 1,400 r. p. m." with no power value.
   p. 9 separately gives 338. The same page says each camshaft has 24 cams
   and is driven by bevel gears "from the cam shaft"; both apparent technical
   errors remain. The footnote cautions that much data concerns the airplane
   engine and was taken from a Signal Corps book.

4. pp. 46, 78, 105, 113: inlet tappet clearance differs. p. 46 prints
   0.014-0.16; Plate 50 on p. 78 and the p. 113 summary give .013-.016;
   p. 105 gives .014-.016. The missing zero/apparent lower-limit disagreement
   has not been harmonized.

5. p. 72: grade capability is explicitly "50 degrees - degrees not per cent"
   and "grades of 50 degrees uphill and down." The degree value is preserved.

6. p. 80: bush dimensions use "0.875-0.0005" and "1.125-0.0005." The source's
   hyphens have not been interpreted as a different tolerance convention.

7. p. 104: "Remove the spark plug from the flywheel of No. 6 L cylinder."
   The unusual flywheel reference remains unresolved.

8. p. 115: both asbestos and leather clutch facings are mentioned; the notes
   do not establish whether these describe variants or a source inconsistency.

9. p. 136: road-track driving-wheel diameter is "23.031" without a unit.
   p. 130 gives 39.237 inches outside and 32.75 inside for that wheel, while
   23.031 inches is the chain-sprocket diameter on pp. 130 and 132. The p. 136
   label/value combination is retained, not treated as a reconciled dimension.

10. p. 169: a battery instruction ends "having a specific gravity" without
    giving the value. Different 15/16-ampere limits also remain as printed.

11. pp. 171, 180: ammunition loads differ: 182 common + 26 smoke and 21,000
    machine-gun rounds on p. 171; 184 Q.F. + 24 case-shot and 13,200 machine-gun
    rounds on p. 180. The latter is part of a tentative equipment list approved
    March 7, 1918; do not assume all entries describe one finalized load.

12. pp. 172, 175, 182: machine-gun counts/locations are not reconciled. p. 175
    says seven total, five in the main turret, then lists one front, one rear,
    and two on each side, plus one in each hull side door. That enumeration
    does not agree with the stated five/seven counts.

13. p. 176: ventilation table prints "0.900 cubic feet" at 1,400 r.p.m.; prose
    gives 900 cubic feet per minute at "1.400 r.p.m." Decimal punctuation and
    the incomplete table unit remain unchanged.

14. p. 177: "26-pounder Q. F. Hotchkiss 23-caliber" runs together the quantity
    2 and the 6-pounder designation. It should not be read as a 26-pounder gun.

15. pp. 191, 194, 196, 198: the project flags unusual hardware descriptions:
    p. 191 cover-stud nut 3/4 inch and screw 3/4 inch-16; p. 194 gear bolt
    11/16 inch with 1 5/8 inches between head and nut; p. 196 "stud nut" and
    p. 198 "drain tube bolt nut" each with a 1/16-by-1/2-inch description.
    These remain source readings, not validated fastener specifications.

16. p. 200: the short stud is printed 2 7/10 inches, with a separate
    parenthetical 1 7/16-inch dimension. The unusual denominator 10 was checked
    against the scan and must not be silently changed to 16.

17. p. 203: piston 13149 is described as "20.5 per cent low compression."
    The meaning of this percentage has not been resolved.

18. p. 229, M-2848: "Ball bearing 40 m. m.; bore, 110 m. m.; O. D., 27 m. m
    wide." The sequence attaches dimensions/labels ambiguously; no reordering
    into a presumed bearing specification has been applied.

PART NUMBERS, QUANTITIES AND LEGENDS

19. pp. 86-87: nut lock 8636 versus 8536, and strainer 8220 versus 8230.
    p. 109 uses 8345 for both gasket and bearing retainer.

20. p. 111, Plate 67 ref. 5: the legend reads SH-198B; the drawing reads
    SH193B. Both are preserved.

21. pp. 122, 125: legend ref. 14 is "Striking stud" on p. 122 and "Striking
    rod" on p. 125. p. 125 repeats ref. 18, both times M-255. p. 140 assigns
    M-1472 to both refs. 3 and 7. p. 162 assigns ref. 15 to both Pinion flange
    and Clutch plate.

22. pp. 131, 133, 139: 20297-D89 becomes 2097-D89 on p. 139; no digit added.
    p. 137 ref. 3 prints 1264 without the M prefix.

23. p. 184: distinct O-38-21192 and Q-38-21192 identifiers are retained.
    M-3015 has the unusual paired quantities (L.H.) 2 and (R.H.) 1.

24. Repeated or anomalous nomenclature identifiers remain as printed:
    p. 205 two identical 990C / 1 / Starter spring rows; p. 207 duplicate
    M-335; p. 209 repeated SH158D and SH403B; p. 213 repeated SH980C,
    982F/G/H without SH, and SH894E amid SH984-series entries; p. 215 repeated
    M-1756 and M-1766, with M-3789 before M-3788; p. 225 G-20793 for both a
    rifle-clip spring and long set screw, plus repeated M-2735; p. 227 M-2437,
    two M-2626 rows, a second M-2791, and M-3812; p. 235 repeated M-3311.

25. pp. 223, 233, 235: differing revolver-hole quantities (11, 10, 12, 24),
    and differing inlet/outlet louver-blade and distance-piece quantities,
    remain unreconciled. p. 231 K2-20793 has one quantity, 5, covering two
    description lines for 4-inch and 8-inch chain lengths.

26. Missing fields/numbering are intentional transcriptions of source gaps:
    p. 39 Peephole ref. 3 has no part number; p. 122 omits refs. 19, 45, 51,
    65; p. 156 omits ref. 5; p. 157 omits ref. 11. The nomenclature has
    unnumbered hardware and numbering gaps, including those on pp. 217,
    219 and 221. p. 223 M-2188 has neither description nor quantity.
    Quantities remain blank for 13443 (p. 199), SH981H (p. 211), SH982E and
    SH985F (p. 213), M-3117 (p. 231), and M-858/M-874 (p. 233). p. 235's
    drain cock has no part number. Printed x quantities are not blanks or 1s.

27. p. 239: P-30 has quantity 1 and description "5 Casing"; the unexplained
    5 is retained. The mileage equipment description prints "26 DLh" and
    "revs. to register 1 meter" without editorial normalization.

CROSS-REFERENCES, CAPTIONS AND INDEX

28. p. 7 groups Plate 107, "Third brush regulation," under Chapter X despite
    its electrical subject. p. 20 captions Plate 11 "EPICUCLIC CUP," whereas
    the plate list says "Epicyclic cup." p. 99 captions Plate 65 "FAN BEVEL
    GEAR DRIVE" amid water-pump discussion.

29. Unresolved references include "see fig. 16" (p. 69), "Figure 33" (p. 79),
    "Referring to page -" with no number (p. 120), and Figure 165 / Diagram
    164 / blue print 164 (pp. 167-168). p. 110 points to p. 111 although the
    numbered sectional view is on p. 112.

30. p. 54 has no visible printed Plate 38 label; p. 212's curves have no
    printed Plate 126 label between Plates 125 and 127. None was invented.

31. Index gaps: "Brake, track" (p. 242) and "Engine-oil specifications"
    (p. 244) lack page references. p. 246 has four "Inspection, disassembling
    and" entries, references 70/86/91/99, without subjects to distinguish them.

32. Index conflicts/repetitions: "Fittings and equipment, miscellaneous"
    refers to 177 (p. 245), but its reversed entry to 176 (p. 246).
    "Progress of drive" refers to 13 (p. 247), versus 120 under "Drive,
    progress of" (p. 244). Repeated entries are also retained for Crank shaft
    (94/101), machine-gun locations (175/182), road-track adjusting wheel
    (138/23), driving wheel (23/133/137), upper rollers (19/18), Steering
    (15/148), and removal of roller pinion (135/144). Multiple references
    can describe different discussions; they are not all established errors.

33. Retained wording that can affect interpretation includes "which measures
    with the track links" (p. 14), "cardan shift" (p. 15), "lock hand"
    (p. 42), cables "sent sharply" (p. 67), "new brushed" (p. 70),
    "felt-packing washing" (p. 79), "soft plate" / "Inspect it from the shaft"
    (p. 80), "lack screw holes" (p. 81), "unit No. 8151" after "nuts" (p. 83),
    "tope tweed packing" (p. 100), "tapped clearance" (p. 106), "track
    adjusting steel" (p. 136), "clutch face rises enormously" (p. 164),
    "sear brushes" (p. 170), "taped and threaded" (p. 183), "Ball-crank"
    (p. 188), "adjusting eye belt" (p. 190), "oil hold plug" (p. 196),
    "Dodent plunger" / "Locking belt for pin" (p. 207), and "Operating belt"
    / "Poaking piece" (p. 223). Intended replacements have not been asserted.
    The source also has lesser spelling/punctuation anomalies; their full
    record remains in the project review notes.
