# Batch 11 visual review — printed pages 201–220

All 20 new source pages were inspected. Native text was rebuilt from explicit
part-number/quantity mappings and source-corrected description lines, with OCR
used as a coordinate scaffold. All 20 new PDF pages were rendered with Poppler
at 144 dpi and visually inspected. Render hashes and the PDF binding are in
render_validation.json. Read REVIEW_NOTES.md for retained source anomalies.

  quantities, headings, row order, source breaks and blank cells checked.
- Page 201: electrical/flywheel/generator/governor sections; source signature;
  sixteenths in the generator key checked against enlarged source.

- Page 211: 11/16 bearing width, shared air-tube blocks and blank H quantity.
  Five tube baselines reconstructed from source word positions; no overlaps.
- Page 213: nested component lines, C–E brace, blank quantities and plain 5
  dimensions. The shared block aligns with the normalized left-hand rows.
- Pages 215, 217, 219: petrol/hull lists checked, including source repetitions,
  numbering gaps, original order and floor-plate numbers lost by draft OCR.

## Artwork

- Pages 202, 204, 206, 208, 210, 212, 214, 216, 218 and 220: all artwork/caption
  placements checked against the source. All 13 new image assets are included.
- Page 204: expanded Plate 120 crop to retain the whole M1473 label. Plate 121
  retains all engineering leaders and edge labels.
- Pages 208 and 218: native captions read bottom to top to match the source.
  Artwork itself is neither rotated nor dewarped.
- Page 212: original chart grids, labels and curves retained as raster. No
  visible source plate number; no invented Plate 126 label.
- Small isolated marks in blank paper on Plate 121, the upper p212 chart and
  Plate 130 are repaired by deterministic interpolation of neighboring paper
  tone. This avoids hard white rectangles in areas with a gutter gradient.
- Photograph/halftone texture and some paper discoloration remain. No detail
  is generated or redrawn. Font and physical trim remain provisional.

whole-description frames do not require that degree of compression.
