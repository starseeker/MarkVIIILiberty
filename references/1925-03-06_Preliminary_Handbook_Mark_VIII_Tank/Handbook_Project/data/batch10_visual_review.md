reviewed. Contact-sheet review covered all twenty pages, with enlarged inspection
of body/list hierarchy, table columns and fractions, grouped quantities, plate
boundaries and the rotated caption. Independent proofreading remains pending.

  Note small capitals, common fractions and the three inline slash fractions.

  two handed quantities for M-3015. Extra vertical room prevents these quantities
  from touching the next row.
- Pages 185, 188–192, 194, 196, 198–200: twelve native nomenclature tables,
  765 source rows, column identities, blank cells, x quantities, wrapped cells,
  the No numbers group and repeated headings.
- Source detail checks include p185 P-406 quantity 12, p188 5/32 split pin,

  both ½-inch cock dimensions, and p200 2 7/10 versus 1 7/16.

  body baseline. Fraction bars sit between the numerator and denominator; the
  compact arrangement avoids collisions with adjacent rows. The horizontal
  fitting report includes small two-digit fraction components just below 85%;
  no table descriptions or body lines require that much compression.
- Pages 186–187, 193, 195 and 197: all eight new plate crops retain original
  engineering detail and internal lettering. Captions and plate labels remain
  native. The vertical CONTROL SYSTEM caption and p193 printer signature were
  checked. Paper normalization leaves some original texture and shading.
- All 180 earlier PDF pages are pixel-identical to v9 at 144 dpi. The exact
  180-page source SLA checkpoint is included. Object/style serialization changes
  are documented in preservation_validation.json.

No native text overflows, unexpected extra lines, text mismatches, missing PDF
