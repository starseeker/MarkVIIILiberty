# Water-shim witness refinement

The whole unmasked shim footprint extends into the intended mating clearance:
0.45 mm radially over the 56 mm receiver opening and 0.05 mm over each of four
4.8625 mm stud holes. The actual missing volume is 40.9447697533 mm³, entirely in
those five regions. The prior qualified water-pump case and both receiver trials
produce the same result; the new screw recess does not reach the joint plane.

The refined witness checks every part of the saved shim footprint outside these
existing design clearances. A negative control extends the screw-head recess
through the water joint; that must remove required bearing land and be rejected.
These are mechanical fit allowances from the existing water assembly, not new
historical dimensions or relaxed numerical tolerances. The original failed
report and checker are retained here; the geometric diagnostic is ../../shim_diagnostic.
