# Countersink sign diagnosis

The first saved-native checker expected a positive 35-degree cone half-angle.
All eighteen lining cones report -35 degrees: their radius decreases along the
outward hole axis. The correction checks the signed -35-degree half-angle and
requires that outward axis, preserving the intended wide opening on the drum
side. No geometry or angular tolerance changed. Initial failures and actual
surface parameters are retained here; later native checks live in the pipeline.
