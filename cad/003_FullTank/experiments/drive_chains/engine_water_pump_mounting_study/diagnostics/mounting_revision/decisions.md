# Mounting reconstruction decisions

The source scan SNL239 was directly inspected. Four studs are 3/8 x 1-31/32
inch, with 21/32 inch US Standard and 5/8 inch SAE threaded ends. Each has one
special LQ198A nut, one special LQ166A washer and one 3/32 x 5/8 inch cotter.
Nominal smooth thread envelopes retain these stock lengths. Full embedding of
the case end is selected, not measured. It leaves 33.3375 mm outside the case,
including a 17.4625 mm unthreaded part before the outer thread.

The reconstructed stack is shim .25 + retainer flange5 + gasket.4 + body
flange14 =19.65 mm, followed by washer1.5875 and nut9.525. Thus the nut starts
21.2375 mm from the case face, within the outer thread, and ends30.7625 mm;
2.575 mm of stud extends beyond it. Nut crown and pin geometry respect the
source cotter stock length. Exact flange, nut and washer shapes remain estimates.
No external historical dimensional qualification is claimed.

Pitch radius84, flange radius74 and ear radius12 provide pads outside the
72mm estimated chamber bulge. This is a substantive casting change; source
camera/scale registration remains pending. A diagonal ear estimate collided
with two outlet barrels; a cardinal estimate cleared the outlets but collided
with the drain plug and lower-drive retaining screw. Rotating ears and outlets
together22.5 degrees is the next candidate. Its validity requires full checks.
The displayed22.5 degrees is a construction choice, not historical precision.
SNL14 explicitly states its water outlets are transposed, preventing use of
that picture as an absolute installed clocking datum.

Tangential outlet centerlines follow the annular chamber radial centerline,
closer to SNL17/LIB79 than the crossing barrel approximation. Exact matched
radii caused the Boolean union to lose connected material and were rejected.
The next trial uses outer20/inner15 against torus minor21/16, a small throat
transition within unprinted casting uncertainty. This changes physical material;
it must not be described as a material-equivalent tolerance fix or a measured
volute. The isolated unclocked full body had native tolerance1.000001e-6 mm.
Clocked final body and STEP still require qualification.

Fluid voids must be cut after adding thick rear mounting stock, but before
adding the blind cover-stud receiving bosses. Cutting the fluid void last can
remove those bosses' surrounding/bottom material. New independent checks test
both the blind case receivers and all eight blind cover receivers.

Socket checks cover an estimated installed cylindrical envelope only; they do
not establish an insertion/removal path or tool swing. Pose/service validation
remains a later workflow phase. Drain lock wire remains absent at this stage.
