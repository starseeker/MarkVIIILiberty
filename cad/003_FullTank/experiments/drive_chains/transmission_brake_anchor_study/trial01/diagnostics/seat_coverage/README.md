# Composite cylindrical bearing surface

The first saved-artifact check reported incomplete foot support. Its candidate
face filter required every band face to span the entire bracket face axially.
That discarded two small cylindrical patches created by filling the former
lining-rivet upset pockets, even though those patches are part of the same
valid backing solid and lie on the same analytic cylinder.

The retained probe checks the unchanged saved BReps. Including all coincident
cylindrical patches leaves zero uncovered faces and area, for both brackets.
The corrected checker requires complete coverage by their union and retains
the displaced-foot negative control, separate interference tests and unchanged
numerical tolerances. No physical geometry was changed to fix this check.
