# Next mechanism packet — source and interface starting points

This is preparation, not accepted geometry. Complete the standard mechanism before
poses. The forward fitting checkpoint reserves interfaces that may be revised if
better source or mechanism evidence contradicts them.

Original SNL118 was directly inspected again on 24 September 2026. Each high-speed
lever assembly contains **one M355B left member, one M355A right member, and four
⅜ × 1⅜ inch button-head rivets**. The two complete levers therefore require four
members and eight rivets. Left/right describes the members of each assembled lever;
it is not permission to install one member on each side of the tank.

The SNL137 M356 entry identifies four free-end pins but supplies no printed stock
size. HB154 explicitly requires removing their cotters and drifting out the pins.
The M363 anchor-pin cotter's printed 3/16 × 2 inch size must not silently become an
M356 specification. Establish an explicit free-pin/cotter approximation and retain
that distinction if no drawing supplies dimensions.

HB151/154 constrains the mechanism: the adjusting screw passes through the brake
lever and is pinned to the lower short band. The lever's other upper connection
joins the long band; its lower arm joins the rear control rod. The adjusting nut
compresses the spring between the lever and the shoulder on the adjusting screw.
This arrangement differs from the previously populated low/track brake mechanism.

Transcribed SNL274 and the transmission composition identify two MX78A washers
with 11/16 inch bore and two MX78B washers with 13/16 inch bore. Reinspect original
SNL274 before finalizing their geometry. Bore assignment, outside diameters, stock,
shoulder and spring seats must be reconciled together; do not substitute the
low/track swivel and spring layout automatically.
