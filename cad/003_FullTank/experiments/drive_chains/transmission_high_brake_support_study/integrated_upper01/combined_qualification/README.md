# Combined high-speed support qualification

The saved development model now passes the combined local static gates. Its
geometry and counts are unchanged: 3,165 physical occurrences, 542 definitions
and 336 assembly groups. The [qualification](../qualification.json) permits
continued reconstruction with documented approximations; it does not qualify
full standard-tank coverage, structural performance or the complete service path.

The six-stage deterministic plan reuses 38 bound scoped receipts from the three
construction increments. It checks every inherited definition's preservation
chain, then independently tests the final combination:

| Gate | Result |
|---|---|
| Evidence | 569 assertions pass, including 528 inherited definition chains. |
| Relocated native | All 3,165 links and composed frames bind to local definitions; 15 checks cover the 14 affected closed definitions. |
| Combined material | 431 selected full-solid comparisons pass for all 45 affected occurrences against the retained powertrain. |
| Combined STEP | 14 definition and 45 installed-solid comparisons pass; no geometric acceptance limit changed. |
| Source | Complete port brake and two case sections compared in the unchanged HB133 registration. |
| Standard context | All 45 final components retain current context evidence; standard native files were rehashed and intervening geometry/frame preservation checked. |

The [source overlay](source/support_source.png) exposes remaining differences in
lever form, case sections, fastener heads and stop folds. The source does not
identify one physical section depth for all its details. No camera refit was
needed, and visual review does not independently validate dimensions scaled
from that same illustration.

The large receiving case retains the qualified refined mass integration with
its failed default measurements. Native/STEP material and tolerance criteria
remain unchanged. Individual stock variations and fresh reconstruction are
bound from the previous checks, avoiding redundant rebuilds.

The initial five-stage execution completed in about 113 seconds of recorded
stage time. Adding the standard-context binding reused those five passed stages
and executed the new stage only. These figures exclude orchestration/hash time
and supervising-assistant usage; they are not an end-to-end speedup estimate.
Pipeline records and the final six-stage plan are retained in
[../pipeline_evidence](../pipeline_evidence/).

Two MX60 locations, the later lock-washer alternative, exact hidden profiles and
full removal/tool handling remain open. Operating control construction continues
