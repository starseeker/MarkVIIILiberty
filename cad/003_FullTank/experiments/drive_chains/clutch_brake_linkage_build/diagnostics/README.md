# Brake integration trial evidence

The first trial passed 64 STEP comparisons but failed two of 89 independent checks
and 15 material pairs. The second passed all 89 independent checks and 64 STEP
comparisons but had six material interferences. Complete trial natives and renders
remain in .work/clutch-brake-linkage/first_fit and second_fit.

The retained second-fit collision locations distinguish the lower cotter eye
touching the crown-slot floor from its straight legs. The final crown slot is
0.5mm deeper; source split-pin diameter and leg length are unchanged. The anchor
web toe rises 3mm, and the carrier routes below the rod/nuts. Earlier corrections
separate plate routes from the auxiliary shaft, create flat crank-port seats,
extend the two clevis throats, and move the spring's first adjuster stack clear of
the anchor rivets. A later trial passed all 112 independent checks and 64 STEP
comparisons but retained two small mounting overlaps. The web toe is now trimmed
at the bracket's flat mounting plane below the plate's top edge. The carrier's
estimated return moves 4mm outward, clearing the anchor plate's outer end. This
finishing uses defined joint planes and an explicit return-offset control, not
arbitrary subtraction of neighboring solids. Source hardware sizes are unchanged.
These are revisions to estimated geometry, not relaxed tests.

Collision bounds in the diagnostic can include underlying untrimmed loft surfaces;
the reported common-solid centroid and material volume locate the actual overlap.
The final saved-native check and exchange receipts are the acceptance evidence.
This evidence does not establish historical mounting, motion or structural strength.
