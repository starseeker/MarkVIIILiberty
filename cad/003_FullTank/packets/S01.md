# S01 — upper structures and sponsons

Status: active, partial upper and sponson plate population. Standard assembled
configuration only. Fittings, supports and mount interfaces remain pending;
this packet is not accepted. See [the 39-plate sponson increment](S01-sponsons.md).

## Current geometry

Twenty-six native armor plate/leaf solids replace the three solid installation
blocks for the main enclosure, driver's enclosure and lookout. They cover 21
canonical SNL part identities. The hierarchy is HullStructure / UpperStructure /
MainEnclosure, DriverEnclosure, Lookout / individual plate links. Definition
coordinates use their enclosure's local assembly datum; they are not global
vehicle coordinates. Opposite-hand instances currently use separate definitions
where the assembly-reference geometry differs, while retaining shared survey IDs.

## Controlling evidence and assumptions

HB35 controls main shell length 3,136.9 mm, width 1,028.7 mm and height 571.5 mm;
16 mm wall and 6 mm roof armor. Driver dimensions are 685.8 × 482.6 × 330.2 mm,
with 6 mm plates. The lookout has a 457.2 mm square clear opening, assumed
parallel 16 mm walls, 6 mm roof and 317.5 mm total height above the main roof.
The roof overhang is inferred. Front/rear main armor thickness is provisionally
transferred from the printed side thickness.

The main shell base is registered to the midpoint of the unchanged SNL2 trace
at pixel (719.5, 237). Printed dimensions control its size. The original trace is
about 2,766 mm long and 658 mm high, so there is a material source disagreement;
`reports/upper_plates.json` records exact differences. The photograph is not
rescaled to conceal it. The resulting lookout roof is at Z = 3,011.322 mm,
112.878 mm below the separate HB9 overall-height figure of 3,124.2 mm. The upper
base registration therefore also remains an open installation issue: do not
claim the printed whole-vehicle height is satisfied. Resolve the original base
pick and roof interface in H01 before fixing that placement. Overall height here measures the shell crown; the closed
roof leaves sit above it by their assumed 6 mm thickness.

HB43 controls the opening sequence, including different port/starboard ordering.
Its 16.812 × 15.812 in side dimension is interpreted as a leaf size; a nominal
1 mm clearance is added at each edge. HB39's 20 × 15.875 in roof opening and
HB43's two 21.5 × 8.875 in leaves are retained separately: the closed leaves
lap the roof around the opening and have a central 1 mm gap. Exact historical
lap, batten and sealing geometry is not established.

HB43's rear viewing-slit and pistol-opening dimensions are provisionally reused
for other such apertures. Other opening sizes and stations are bounded
assumptions. Vertical stations use documented construction fractions in
`upper_parts.py`: main side viewing slits 0.73H, side mount 0.49H, rear side
pistol 0.68H, forward pistol 0.65H, flap center 0.52H, rear corner apertures
0.57H; driver slit 0.64H side / 0.66H front; lookout slit 0.60H. These fractions
are reconstruction choices, not extracted dimensions. Circular roof holes
approximate undimensioned observation/periscope openings.

SNL7 and HB30 inform identifiable topology, front plan chamfers, rear rake,
roof bend and lookout roof overhang. The aft roof uses a planar miter in place
of the photographed formed radius. Wall and roof joints are trimmed butt/miter
approximations. Width, rake, chamfer and stations remain editable reconstruction
parameters, with evidence and bounds in `data/parameters.json`.

The native bodies contain constrained plate-section sketches and pads, followed
by BRep opening/joint-trim features. Regenerate through `manage.py` after changing
authored parameters; manually changing an upstream native pad does not regenerate
the scripted Boolean opening geometry.

## Qualification and visual review

The build checks all plate shapes as single valid solids, source quantities,
spatially overlapping candidate pairs, hollow interior probes and five sampled
armor thicknesses. Qualification also checks native placements, STEP round trips,
relocated external links, independent saved/reopened rebuild, cache reuse and a
10 mm main-width change. That change must move the closed side leaves 5 mm
outward, widen roof halves, keep the driver/lookout unchanged and preserve the
upper plate checks. This is a propagation test, not permission to change printed
historical dimensions.

Generated front, rear and underside inspection views show the actual installed
solids without moving components. HB30 and SNL7 remain qualitative comparisons;
the full SNL2 overlay retains its fixed calibration. No pose variants are created.
