# P02 — selected static poses: scope and source preparation

The user selected a static assembly plus selected hatch, sponson and service
poses, with all identifiable interiors and documented approximations. This is
preparatory evidence for P02; **no posed CAD assembly is delivered yet**.
The user's latest direction defers pose implementation until the standard
assembled geometry, including interiors, is fully populated.

The normal level-ground installation remains the reference configuration.
Alternative poses must retain the same occurrence identities and component
inventory. Detached service covers need named service placements and retain
ownership; a presentation exploded view is recorded separately.

## Candidate pose set

| Arrangement | Source basis | Required geometry/check |
|---|---|---|
| Normal installation, closures shut | SNL2/SNL7; HB30/HB32 | Plate openings, hinge axes, latches and closed contacts. |
| Main roof hatch open | HB39 and Plate30 | Two handed doorplates M2355/M2356, their hinges and overlap. Confirm whether the printed opening is clear or overall before deriving leaf outlines. |
| Selected side access door open | HB39; HB32; SNL82–83 | Upper/lower door pieces remain distinct. Establish how they connect and hinge before treating the door as one moving rigid group. |
| Sponsons withdrawn for transport | HB37–38 | Forward hinge, rear flange fasteners, support roller and circular floor rail. Inspect final stowed clearance; external-equipment transport placement is a separate adjustment. |
| Engine access arrangement | SNL7 and detachable/removable panel identities | M1908 engine roof plate and selected side access panel removed to documented service placements. Check the actual access opening and removal obstructions. |

Opening angles are not yet assigned. Choose them after reconstructing the hinge
and stop geometry; unsupported endpoint angles remain explicit approximations.
Checking two static endpoints does not establish clearance along the movement
between them. Sample the intervening motion if it is claimed usable.

HB39 states side-door dimensions of 28.5 × 41.875 in and top-door dimensions of
20 in longitudinally × 15.875 in transversely. It also describes outward-opening
driver and upper-enclosure viewing plates; these are candidates after the main
access poses, not evidence that every closure shares one hinge arrangement.

HB38 describes sponsons swinging aft about forward hinges after rear flange
bolts are removed. Their support rollers run on arcs centered at those hinges.
The stated roller dimensions are 110 mm OD, 40 mm bore and 27 mm width, with a
3/4 in × 4 in rail supported by three floor brackets. Those relationships should
define the pose, rather than an arbitrary rotation of the current sponson box.

HB30, HB32 and SNL7 were inspected as oblique arrangement references. They
establish useful seams and hardware relationships without a uniform metric image
scale. The H01/S01 component work must supply the physical boundaries and
interfaces before P02 can be accepted.
