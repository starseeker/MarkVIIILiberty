## Route construction and assumptions

The printed 11.154 in pitch (283.3116 mm) is a rigid pin-to-pin chord. The route
solver keeps that pitch and the 78-unit count fixed. It starts from the recorded
SNL Plate 2 outer-track guide, takes its convex outline, insets it to a provisional
pin route and rounds it with an assumed 550 mm radius. A dense periodic spline
represents this construction curve.

The solver marches exact-length chords, varying one uniform curve scale until
the 78th joint meets the first. It then sets the ground datum from the modeled
underside rivet buttons. Every shoe/link unit receives one rigid transform:
no part is stretched to hide the closing error.

The current solution scales the rounded/inset construction by 1.0580907513 and
translates it vertically by 57.6927 mm. Its pin displacement from that **unscaled
construction curve** is 188.648 mm RMS and 302.773 mm maximum. These numbers are
not distances to the scanned outer silhouette. The source-image calibration is
unchanged, and the native-edge overlay retains visible disagreement.

The installed joint angles range from −0.009908° to 28.176469°. These are the
actual static arrangement, not a claim about a continuously permitted movement
range. Curve closure alone does not locate the drive/idler contact circles or
lower roller rails correctly.

SNL Plate 2's provisional idler-axis pick was corrected from pixel (163,350) to
(194,339), at the visible adjusting-shaft center. The new world station is
X=9167.058 mm, Z=1517.638 mm. HB Plates 83, 86 and 87 were inspected for the
installed drive, drive components and idler section and added to the source lock.

## Open wheel/contact conflict

HB p. 130 explicitly states a road-track drive-wheel **outside** diameter of
39.237 in and 35 teeth. HB p. 133 gives 3.735 in tooth pitch; HB p. 119 describes
the drive reduction as 9:37. These statements and HB p. 137's shoe pitch do not
yet establish one consistent contact geometry. The outside dimension must not
silently be relabeled as a root diameter or the shoe pitch altered.

The current wheel annuli are layout representations. Their single coarse rims,
widths and transverse placement do not reproduce the two-rim wheel assemblies or
demonstrate contact with the paired track bars. The visible gap in the review
views remains a finding for R02/R03. Resolve or explicitly bound these interfaces
before accepting a complete running-gear assembly.

## Checks and authoritative records

- `data/patterns.json` selects the two closed 78-unit patterns.
- `lib/track_path.py` solves and records the route.
- `lib/track_validation.py` checks source composition, production quantities,
  every shared pin, closing joints, native NURBS retention and material overlap.
- `build/reports/track_path.json` records construction and residuals.
- `build/reports/track_components.json` records exact checked quantities/contacts.
- `build/reports/validation.json` records native/STEP, relocation, rebuild,
  cache and parameter-change results for the matching input fingerprint.

The nominal installation checks 1,958 spatially selected material-intersection
pairs without overlap. Internal fit is checked once for the identical rigid unit
template; every cross-unit candidate on the port loop is checked. Reuse on the
starboard loop is conditional on verifying the same definitions and exact rigid
transverse translation, plus disjoint track bounding boxes. Wheel and support
contacts are outside that self-interference result.

The native assembly and component STEP retain all 2,964 parts. Whole-vehicle
previews omit small track hardware for readability; the three-unit close views
retain every part. The renderer reuses definition meshes and transforms them at
each installed placement. This is a preview optimization, not a geometry change.

Native placement checks compare each installed transform with the authored datum
chain. Measuring all 2,991 reopened occurrences found maximum translation error
0.000000178751 mm and maximum rotated-axis error 0.000000001162. The checks allow
0.000001 mm and 0.00000001 respectively and report actual maxima. Shape bounds
alone are unsuitable for this check: a rotated split pin showed a 0.000036 mm
optimal-bound variation while both Boolean differences were zero.

Final coverage still requires qualified wheel/support geometry, refined track
forms, hull and sponson components, all selected interiors, hardware and the
documented static hatch, sponson and service poses.

## Integration revision — standard hull plate population

The first H01 plate integration found 80 nominal roof/track overlaps on the two
sides, concentrated near upper units 41–44. The closure-only route lacked a
physical hull constraint. Its previous source calibration, pitch, count and
part definitions remain intact, while `track_path` now adds a separately named
bounded clearance correction before solving chord closure.

The correction is vertical, with 60 mm peak before scaling, center X = 4,200 mm
and longitudinal Gaussian sigma 800 mm. A smoothstep in source-curve Z from
800 to 1,500 mm activates it only on the upper run. The lower straight contact
segment receives no local correction before the existing global closure scale
and ground registration. These are explicit reconstruction controls arising
from model integration, not new historical measurements or a photographic fit.

The qualified nominal reconstruction uses uniform scale 1.0577179103 and ground
registration 57.322449 mm.
Pin residuals against the **uncorrected** rounded/inset source construction are
191.722 mm RMS and 300.847 mm maximum. They do not measure direct silhouette
agreement with source pixels. Bends range from −0.615433° to 28.186605°; all
78 chords retain the printed pitch. The modest reverse curvature is included
in nominal component contact checking; continuous motion remains unqualified.
The current generated reports supersede earlier nominal-route metrics in this
packet. H01 checks exact hull contacts in the integrated build and in the
track-pitch trial, in addition to the existing track self-contact checks.
The delivered native build passes all 1,958 selected track self-contact pairs and
1,135 hull contact pairs; the +0.5 mm pitch variant passes closure, all 3,088
installed placements and 1,111 hull contact pairs. Full historical track fit and
wheel/rail engagement remain unqualified.
