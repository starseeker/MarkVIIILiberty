`lib/roller_geometry.py` computes each station against the closed track without

conservative placement construction, not a substitute for native rail checks.

Positive Y is port. The pin and plug reverse on port so that the plug is outboard.
The two upper rollers reverse relative to one another to orient their flanges

locking-washer helical set are unmodeled.

## Native construction and installation

`lib/roller_parts.py` builds constrained native revolution sketches for rollers,
tubes, bushes, rings and spring plates. Lower and upper shoulders use cubic
B-spline curves. Separate analytic features add the pin's blind oil gallery,
radial holes and end flats. A helical sweep provides three spring turns, with
axial rise adjusted so the native solid's total loaded length equals HB141.
This adjustment accounts for the swept wire's end sections; it does not infer
ground ends or working force.

Nested source assemblies retain the base rotating stack, spring stack, pin
assembly and staple/nut/washer assemblies. Each plain lower stack has 19 leaves, a spring stack 22 and an upper stack 21 including its two M2092 angles. Native reusable definitions live in
`build/native/library/RunningGear.FCStd`, linked through the RunningGear subsystem
into `build/native/MarkVIII.FCStd`. Keep the complete native directory together.
Authoritative JSON and Python inputs require regeneration after editing.

`lib/roller_validation.py` independently checks source records and quantity
scopes, proves repeated stacks have identical relative placements/definitions
before reusing six representative contact checks, and checks all external and
inter-station candidates against actual installed solids. It records actual
wheel-to-native-rail distances, controlled dimensions and ring stock volume.
Full qualification also reopens STEP/native files, relocates the dependency tree,
rebuilds independently, checks cache reuse and perturbs roller diameter and track
pitch. Artificial trials are not historical dimensional revisions.

## Original roller-stage verification record and disposition

The corrected plain/spring/upper prototypes each passed internal material checks.
At that release, twenty-one record/renderer tests passed, including rejection of a missing HB upper
tube and verification that source station X/Z picks remain unchanged when track
pitch moves installed roller stations and dependent hull openings.

Integrated build, saved-native qualification and visual review pass for this
partial roller subset. R02 as a whole remains active.
The fit validator uses OCC optimal bounds with triangulation disabled: the
FreeCAD display mesh otherwise underestimates these curved roller and spring
bounds. The initial diameter-check failure was a measurement-method issue, not
a change to the printed diameter.
Visual inspection of the generated transverse section confirms the distinct
roller, tube, bush, pin/gallery and spring components. The spring-plate contour
uses a tapered inner web and flat outer flange; SNL29 shows a more continuously
formed contour. This remains an explicit shape approximation, as do the roller
shoulder curves and washer/fastener forms.

The machine-readable results are in `build/reports/rollers.json` and
`build/reports/validation.json`, with HB88/89 and SNL29 comparisons under
`build/comparisons/`. Packet acceptance remains open until the support, drive,
idler and adjustment families are also populated and checked.
