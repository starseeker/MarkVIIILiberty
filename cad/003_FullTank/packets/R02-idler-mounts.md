## Evidence and allocation

records the resulting corner-diameter discrepancy. HB140 supplies the copper

were inspected and added to the source lock; no source text or survey identity

two M1409 bushes, two M1477 nuts, two M1475 locking screws and two Q52C plugs.

brackets, plates, guards, main screws, washers and copper plugs respectively.
SNL201:007 gives sixteen ¾ × 1¾-inch cap screws, four per bracket.

SNL170:003 allocates only five ½ × 1⅝-inch rivets to each outer M1962B/A and
M1484 pairing. Ten are installed. The whole-vehicle total of 28 includes other
uses and is not an idler-only quantity. Four M1484 plates are modeled; inner
plate retention remains unresolved. The eight heads shown around the source
opening cannot be treated as eight of the specified cap screws. Hole placement
is an explicit reconstruction choice, with three rear and two front rivets on
each outer reinforcement plate. The source image does not prove that allocation.

width, against an inboard attachment land. The rim running envelope is unchanged.
Diaphragm outer webs now have a flat central trough and curved shoulders.
Five X and one Y retain separate identities but share the same inferred form.

rim/bushing gap. Neither the numerical search bound nor the guard opening is
claimed as historical adjustment travel. The foremost roller retains its
separate 45 mm rearward correction, exceeding the original six-pixel allowance

The 701.675 mm shaft length is an unconfirmed transfer from the driving shaft;
the 112.6236 mm bearing diameter is inferred from the printed bushing bore.
End journals, guide slot, cast neck/foot, reinforcement contour, oil galleries,

envelopes represent the screws, nut and tapped joints; threads are not modeled.

front-upper hull plates, replacing the former unrelated 95 mm round hole.

Cap-head positions and fore rivets were adjusted within the inferred layout to
clear the bracket neck and neighboring hull skirt. These are geometric repairs,
not newly discovered source dimensions.

## Verification scope

Source composition is checked independently against frozen SNL records.
Installation IDs scope shared Q52C/wheel definitions, avoiding accidental roller
or future drive-wheel double counts. Every spatially selected idler/internal
and idler/other-physical pair receives an OCC material-intersection check.
Native checks also measure bush running gaps, rotating boss clearances, nut and
washer seats, cap/head seats, oil-plug seats, copper saddle contact, reinforcing
plate/hull contacts and both ends of every identified reinforcement rivet.
Rivet upsetting conserves the printed stock volume.

The existing pitch and idler-diameter trials now require fixed support geometry
to remain unchanged while the wheel/shaft station moves. An additional +2 mm
shaft-length trial requires longer shaft/locking screws, opposite 1 mm oil-plug
end movements, preserved bearing contacts and unchanged unrelated geometry.
The full saved-native delivery, STEP round trips, relocation and independent
rebuild/cache qualification must pass before this increment is reported delivered.

Remaining work includes exact wheel/link engagement, cast and guard profiles,
inner reinforcement retention, lower roller supports, upper attachments/covers,
drive assemblies and the remaining tank exterior/interior systems.
