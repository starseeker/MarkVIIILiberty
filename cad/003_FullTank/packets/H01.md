# H01 — main hull and primary track structures

Status: active, partial standard shell. This plate set is not the complete hull
BOM or a finished historical reconstruction. Pose variants remain deferred.

## Geometry and identity scope

Seventy-seven individual native plate solids replace the central-hull solid
reference envelope and both track-frame solid references. They represent 63
source identities. The two outer track-route wires remain reference geometry.
The hierarchy owns the plates under HullStructure / MainHull / PortHullShell,
StarboardHullShell, HullFloors, HullRoofs and HullClosures. The enclosing frame
retains drive-axis X and ground Z. The source-plane coordinates are hull-local;
the plate sections include their explicit transverse installation coordinates.

`data/hull_source_rows.json` records independent SNL quantity rows. The validator
resolves each original mark through the immutable survey, checks the row belongs
to that identity, reads its stated count and compares actual modeled occurrences.
A source row's count is used for this selected part only; it does not establish
that all required hull identities have been included. Shared marks retain their
canonical identity across handed definitions.

Native bodies contain constrained source-section sketches and pads followed by
BRep seam, aperture and joint trims. They are closed solids, not meshes or fused
whole-tank lumps. The final shape updates through the scripted build; manual edits
to an upstream native pad do not regenerate the scripted Boolean features.

## Evidence and geometric decisions

Read HB9, 11–12, 35–43 and 141 directly from the frozen survey. Inspected SNL1,
3/4, 7 and 10, plus HB6 and the existing SNL2 calibrated longitudinal image.
SNL1/7 provide visible seam topology and handed piece-mark families. Perspective
photographs are qualitative evidence; no global metric scale is fitted to them.

HB141 gives 22.25 in (565.15 mm) between inner and outer shell plating along the
lower roller line. The model treats that as a clear gap centered on each printed
HB9 track center. This centering is an assumption, not a recovered section. The
roller-facing planes are at |Y| = 600.075 and 1,165.225 mm. With 12 mm inner
shell stock the narrow front/fuel interior is 1,176.15 mm wide; the broad floors
span the outer walls at 2,330.45 mm. Front-wing and skirt thicknesses differ, so
local cabin-facing surfaces do not all coincide with the 12 mm reference face.
The lower gap itself remains independent of local thickness.

The H-in-plan description and figures inform the broad fighting/engine region,
narrow front/fuel portions, forward inner wing walls and inner skirts under the
broad floor. There is no full-height inner wall through the fighting and engine
rooms. The exact transverse transition and corner joints remain inferred.

HB35 controls nominal 12 mm sides, 10 mm front wings and skirts, 6 mm ordinary
roof, 10 mm fuel roof and 16 mm rear fuel backplate. Unlocated floor thickness is
an explicit bounded 6 mm assumption. Skirt thickness is transferred to inner
skirts provisionally. The flat floor underside uses HB9's 527.05 mm ground
clearance. HB11 places the engine back wall at X = 1,425.575 mm and its front at
4,397.375 mm, with the drive axis at zero. These printed controls replace the
nearest provisional image picks for the relevant interfaces.

The retained SNL2 hull outline is polygonal. Internal vertical seam stations use
inferred image columns 400, 510, 650, 790, 1150, 1300 and 1630; the engine bay's
ends instead use HB11. The side door is centered at column 860 and the removable
engine side leaf at 1375. The front diagonal seam joins image (108,340) and
(400,465). The sponson opening spans columns 510–790 and rows 270–440. These are
bounded reconstruction choices derived from SNL1/7 topology, not recovered
manufacturing profiles; code and calibration retain the exact choices.

The skirt upper edge uses inferred front/rear contour points and the printed
floor plane through the broad central region. Its first pixel-based version
crossed the floor by small amounts and was replaced with an explicit floor joint.
Inner wing tops terminate at the deck underside where the deck covers them;
the sloping front plate is trimmed against the driver's roof strips. These are
butt/miter joint approximations. The actual angles, laps and riveted connections
remain to be modeled. No positive-volume plate overlap is accepted.

Eight numbered floor pieces are installed in sequence. The first two follow an
inferred sloping front floor, then six divide the flat central floor. The actual
seam positions and narrow-to-broad transition are unmeasured. Roof plates follow
a level forward deck and sloping engine/fuel surfaces. Folded sections preserve
normal thickness with mitered inner intersections; bend radii are unlocated.
The source upper-shell placement and overall-height conflict recorded in S01
remains open. Some roof-to-side edges and missing roof/angle pieces still leave
local mating gaps; zero overlap is not a claim of a watertight or complete hull.

## Doors, covers and openings

HB39's 28.5 × 41.875 in door is interpreted as the upper leaf. HB43's 59.875 in
height is provisionally interpreted as the combined upper/lower height, giving
an 18 in lower leaf. Each side has a source-identified closed upper and lower
solid. This interpretation is unproved. The 29.875 in HB43 opening width exceeds
the modeled 28.5 in leaves plus 2 mm edge clearance by 32.925 mm; a framed-opening
scope might explain it, but it is not silently declared resolved. The modeled
sill is 30 mm above the floor underside; the seam has a nominal 1 mm gap.

Side-door mount holes, front adjuster penetrations, rear drive penetrations,
viewing slits and removable engine side panels have approximate sizes/positions
where no direct dimensions have been located. Covers, mounting rings, fastener
holes and local reinforcing plates remain pending. Both large sponson apertures
stay open for the source-specific sponson assemblies.

The roof contains inlet and outlet apertures with HB40 widths 946.15 mm and
longitudinal lengths 655.955 / 955.675 mm. Their longitudinal stations are
inferred. Blades, flanged guards, spacers and fasteners are a separate outstanding
cooling/ventilation component set, not solid blocks covering the apertures.
The current M1908 engine-access roof plate is closed. Additional roof strips,
mud-chute regions and rear track-roof pieces are not yet populated.

## Verification and remaining work

The hull validator checks the 63 source quantities, all modeled hull/upper plate
candidate intersections and, in the integrated model, physical track contacts.
It independently checks both lower shell gaps, flat-floor underside, six empty
compartment/roller-housing probes and sloped louver aperture probes. Standard
native/STEP/rebuild/relocation checks apply to all delivered solids.

A +10 mm lower-shell-gap trial temporarily widens the test-only input bound:
inner and outer faces must move oppositely by 5 mm, broad floors widen, narrow
front floors shrink and upper enclosures stay unchanged. This is a numerical
propagation trial, not a change to the printed historical dimension. The existing
upper-width trial also checks the new hull interfaces after rebuilding.

Before H01 can be accepted, refine curved outer edges and primary beams/angles,
complete roof strips, bulkheads and doors, floor inspection covers, mud chutes,
front diaphragms and closures, joints, fasteners and mount interfaces. Reconcile
side-door dimension scope, the inner-shell centering assumption and the upper
assembly/overall-height discrepancy. Source quantities for this plate subset do
not waive those remaining components or historical-fit issues.

## First integration with physical tracks

The first full build exposed 80 roof/track-part overlaps on the two sides, around
upper-run units 41–44. The standalone hull checks could not reveal them. The
measured overlap affected M1901 and M1904A/M1915A roof pieces and the inward-facing
link bars, bushes, pins and cotters. The old track route had only source-envelope,
count, pitch and ground constraints; it had not been qualified against actual
hull metal.

The integration iteration adds an explicit bounded upper-route correction before
chord closure: a 60 mm peak vertical Gaussian at X = 4,200 mm with 800 mm sigma,
activated smoothly only on the upper run. The scalar correction is documented
in R03; it does not change the fixed image calibration or printed track pitch.
The roof plates are retained while this provisional route is checked against
all physical hull parts. This choice does not prove the historical roof level
or track path; it resolves a consistency requirement of the developing standard
assembly. See the final integration report for acceptance of the nominal contact
checks and the remaining wheel/rail support work.

Final qualification of this increment passes all 124 native definitions and
3,088 installed placements, 1,135 hull material-pair checks with zero overlap,
STEP round trips, relocated links, independent reopened rebuild, unchanged cache
reuse and all four parameter trials. The pitch variant checks 1,111 hull contact
pairs without overlap. All modeled hull plates remain partial; these results do
not close the remaining H01 component or source-fit work.
