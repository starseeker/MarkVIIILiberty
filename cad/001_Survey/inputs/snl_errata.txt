STANDARD NOMENCLATURE LIST G-13 - MARK VIII (LIBERTY) TANK
March 30, 1928
Errata and unresolved source readings

Companion to 1928-03-30_SNL_G13_MarkVIII_Liberty.pdf.
References use original printed folios, not electronic PDF page numbers.
The reconstruction retains the source readings. Conflicts and tentative
readings below are not authoritative corrections or engineering specifications.
Apparent quantity mismatches may need further interpretation of component uses.

CONFLICTING IDENTIFIERS, DESCRIPTIONS AND DIMENSIONS

1. pp. 44, 110: plain tachometer-drive-shaft bushing is D22920 on p. 44,
   D29920 on p. 110. The flanged companion D22921 is separately corroborated.

2. pp. 46, 249: the left distributor-to-left No. 3 spark-plug cable lists
   spark-plug terminal LQ328A on p. 46. p. 249 distinguishes distributor-end
   LQ328A from spark-plug-end LQ329A. p. 249 also has anomalous cable references
   LQ328A and SH102L that differ from related cable-code forms.

3. pp. 45, 52: left engine-room festoon-lamp cable is 2 ft versus 8 1/2 ft;
   left distributor cable is 12 1/2 ft versus 12 ft in the respective lists.

4. pp. 58, 59, 105, 237: stud LQ196A is 1 9/16 inches on pp. 58, 105, 237,
   but 1 3/16 on p. 59. Note (ge) concerns oversize replacement LQ484A and
   does not resolve two lengths for LQ196A.

5. pp. 67, 79: p. 67 calls LQ296A the lower valve-spring collar and LQ297A
   the upper; p. 79 reverses those descriptions. Orientation is unresolved.

6. pp. 87, 88, 90 versus p. 309: engine headings read "Trego," while the
   later note says "Treco Motor Co." Both source forms remain.

7. p. 115, battery-to-telephone-switch-panel lead: assembly length 24 inches
   and quantity (4), but component cable length 41 inches and quantity (1).

8. pp. 138, 219, SH642B Belleville spring: inside diameter 13/16 inch on
   both pages; outside diameter 1 1/2 inches on p. 138, 1/2 inch on p. 219;
   both appear to say 1 inch thick. The p. 219 outside diameter is smaller
   than its inside diameter. Note (gh) does not resolve the dimensions.

9. pp. 146, 165: piston ring LQ469A is "bottom" in the assembly, "top" in
   its individual entry. Notes (gw)/(gaj) distinguish old/replacement pistons
   but do not settle the ring position.

10. pp. 140-141: D29864 appears as both nut and screw. p. 145 repeats the
    SH169K 3/4-by-12 1/2-inch pipe row twice, each quantity 1 and price .12 P.

11. pp. 223-224: the same platform ammunition-storage assembly has
    identification 18 on p. 223 and 48 on p. 224.

12. pp. 237-238: LQ229A thread length is 1 15/16 inches on p. 237 and
    1 5/16 on p. 238; identification changes from 13154 to 1354. p. 59
    corroborates 1 15/16. The continuation's conflicting values remain.

13. p. 240: LQ166A washer is given as 5/8 inch and MX25 stud assembly uses
    a castle nut described as S.A.E. 1 inch; the project flags conflicts with
    related dimensions without establishing replacements.

14. pp. 274-275: M1405X track-wheel diaphragm has drawing reference 58 on
    p. 274 and 59 on p. 275.

APPARENT QUANTITY DISAGREEMENTS AND AMBIGUOUS NOTATION

15. p. 29: 3/8-by-3/4-inch hexagon bolt quantity 49, but the listed uses
    are rest 5X90 (2) and plate M1033 (9).

16. pp. 82-83: M708 side-door clip component counts differ: left "one,"
    right "two (4)." p. 120 gives left door-lock assembly (4), right (2),
    but bracket/packing-piece components (4) for both.

17. p. 139: A8035 rod-end-pin assembly (4), component (2). pp. 150-152:
    revolver-hole cover quantity 7 versus parenthetical total 5.

18. p. 166: 3/16-by-5/8-inch rivet quantity 273 versus listed uses totaling
    21. Further unreconciled totals flagged by the project: 3/4-by-2 1/8-inch
    rivet 203 (p. 189), first countersunk rivet 1,082 (p. 191), and
    1/4-by-1/2-inch round-head screw 49 (p. 201).

19. pp. 191, 225: platform rivets are 386 on p. 191 and 388 in the p. 225
    component list. p. 211 prints "two LQ65G tube END, short (3)."

20. Quantity notation differs from neighboring entries: M568C assembly 44
    without parentheses (p. 136), piston assembly 12 without parentheses
    (p. 146), and starred M1401 driving-wheel rim 4 without parentheses
    (p. 164). These forms have not been silently regularized.

21. Blank quantities, prices and identifiers remain blank, including the
    two clip quantities on p. 66; telephone fuze fields on p. 97; LQ430A
    quantity on p. 129; M1949 Ordnance cell on p. 146; LQ299A spark-plug
    quantity on p. 156; A6951 chain-ring quantity on p. 165; M1904C quantity
    on p. 230; and clutch-throwout quantity on p. 250. No omitted count is
    inferred from analogous assemblies. review.json lists the other blanks.

UNCERTAIN OR ANOMALOUS CODES AND REFERENCES

22. p. 20: lock-wire code reads "-Q512B" with an unclear/missing initial L.
    Related codes suggest LQ512B, but p. 276 does not repeat its full mark.
    p. 93 strap-fastener code looks like NBIB; NB1B is clear on pp. 107, 223.
    Those cross-references support likely identities, not the disputed glyphs.

23. pp. 48, 149: manufacturer numbers L12339 and L3308, and louver plate
    mark 995B without M, remain as printed despite neighboring patterns.
    p. 237's apparent LA88A contrasts with LQ88A elsewhere and in note (ge).

24. Unresolved rivet-use code variants include:
    p. 167 M3790B versus related M3780B; p. 168 M2373 for both plate/batten;
    p. 172 tentative M6412; p. 173 M308B9, M2102A/B, SH492B;
    p. 174 M2056 without A and M2073A/B; pp. 175-176 beam 1927, angle 2318B,
    bracket M2036 and alternating angle/plate names; p. 177 repeated M1976B
    in left/right combinations; p. 178 M2794A/M2784B among M379x references;
    p. 179 plate M2077; p. 183 M2768A opposite M2786B;
    p. 184 repeated M2017B; p. 185 M2017 without suffix and M2073;
    p. 187 repeated M2007A, bracket M3922 and plate M2733;
    p. 188 M2422 for both batten/strap; p. 190 M3818 and M2000B versus
    M3918/M2008A elsewhere; p. 201 reversed-form "2010M B."
    These are flagged source forms, not a proposed mass renumbering.

25. p. 186: adjacent plate M1977 / angle M2106A and angle M1972A / plate
    M1977 references lack intervening punctuation/quantity. None supplied.

26. Further uncertain codes: SH81H (p. 203, faint H); 8X00 (p. 216, numeric
    first character); SS577B/D/C/E (p. 223, versus SH-prefixed listings);
    L8460 for LQ461A (p. 258); D30365 in D20495 washer references (p. 269,
    blurred final digits); D39536 distributor-cup stud (p. 272).

27. p. 165, A6951: note (a) has no standalone definition in pp. 307-311.
    Note (d) on p. 308 discusses the relevant chain-ring/S-hook replacement
    and is a possible intended reference, not an established correction.

28. p. 261, LQ186A: plate reference reads 141, although the book has only
    34 plates. Plate 14 is plausible but not established. p. 264:
    identification 2 / plate 10 beside the M2370B outlook-turret angle rows
    has uncertain row attachment; the reconstruction follows the rear row.

29. Screw-diameter marks on pp. 112, 119, 205-207 include small plus/minus
    signs beside fractions; their placement is not a modern tolerance
    interpretation. The unusual D26948 thread No. 6-38 on pp. 204, 244 was
    confirmed as printed; that confirms the reading, not the physical thread.
    p. 131's packing length 11 9/16 inches still needs independent checking.
    p. 273 associates a 1-inch plain washer with a 1/4-inch bolt.

PRICES AND WORDING

30. Uncertain prices: M2007/2008 A/B 22.60 (p. 3); M2027 A/B 3.98 (p. 6);
    M1900 8.98 (p. 15); camp-kettle bracket 2.99, possibly 2.98 (p. 37);
    M2848/478 sponson-door roller 1.58, possibly 1.53 (p. 197); removable
    platform assembly 41.68 (p. 225); M367 brake strap 8.20, possibly 3.20
    (p. 229); M3292 oil-tank body 26.60 (p. 245). These are retained readings,
    not independently verified price corrections.

31. Price notation/placement: pyrene-refill bracket ".672 P" (p. 40) is
    anomalous but source-checked; .25 aligns with a stove-bolt row rather
    than connector (p. 70); a mark before M2389's 22.78 is unresolved and
    omitted from the numeric cell (p. 147); 11/16-by-3 1/4-inch rivet .02 P
    is anomalous relative to nearby prices (p. 189); 6.93 aligns with two
    SH958E coupling nuts above the SH958A spindle (p. 219).

32. Apparent source wording includes ANFLE (p. 3, possibly damaged G), two
    OLT headings (p. 32), "lower crack case sump" (p. 76), "large plant
    pinion" (p. 80), "scoup" spring (pp. 134, 219, corroborated literally),
    "engine oiled tank" (p. 156), SEPARTATOR (p. 158), "rear shall storage"
    (p. 223), generator brush "and" rather than "arm" (p. 234), "cam shaft
    locker lever" (p. 248), "ball bearieg WASHER" (p. 250), "distributing
    left" (p. 260), and "stove volt" (p. 268). No technical term was silently
    substituted; lesser punctuation issues remain in the detailed review.

33. Plate 2, marked "Face p. 277," is placed after p. 277 for reading order.
    Its original binding placement and foldout dimensions are unconfirmed.
    Corrected plate images retain uncertainty in original aspect ratio and
    interior geometry; their displayed scale is not an independent dimension.

The latest audit corrected SH40A1D to SH40AD (p. 61), SH976C clip identity
(p. 66), and the first transmission-frame rivet length to 1 1/8 inches
(p. 97). Those transcription errors are already resolved. Repeated SH599A
manifold components were explained by note (go), not treated as an error;
606 radiator tubes and generator-washer identification 8209 were corroborated.
