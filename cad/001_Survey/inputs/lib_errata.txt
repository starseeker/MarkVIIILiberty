THE 12 CYLINDER LIBERTY AERO ENGINE (SEPTEMBER 1918)
Errata and unresolved source readings

Companion to 1918-09_12_Cylinder_Liberty_Aero_Engine.pdf.
Page references are the manual's printed page numbers, not PDF page numbers.
The reconstruction preserves these readings. Suggested interpretations below
are provisional; this is not a corrected engineering specification.

1. p. 7, illustration index: Fig. 65 (early-pattern cam-spindle drive) is
   referenced to p. 88, although the illustration is on p. 78.

2. p. 9, specific power: 424 h.p. per cubic foot is paired with 15.0 h.p. per
   cubic metre; 247 h.p. per square foot is paired with 2.666 h.p. per square
   metre. The printed imperial/metric pairs do not agree as conversions.

3. p. 10, specifications: exhaust-manifold, starting-gear, fuel/oil-rate and
   six-hour-weight fields are blank. The omitted values have not been supplied.

4. p. 31: the lubrication reference says Figs. 24-29, although the subsequent
   sectioned views also include Fig. 30.

5. pp. 44, 46, Zenith carburettor: p. 46 calls the dipping tube M and the
   compensators I; Fig. 38's legend on p. 44 identifies the dipping tube as L,
   the compensator jet as E, and the shroud tube as I. The conflicting keys
   remain unresolved. The L legend also lacks its closing parenthesis.

6. p. 52: "top of the slow-running position" is the printed wording in the
   throttle-barrel description; the project flags it as a possible wording
   error. No replacement term has been established.

7. pp. 96, 105-106: "be drawn off by either of two methods, viz.:" is misplaced
   at the bottom of p. 96, interrupting the throttle instruction continued on
   p. 97. It appears to complete "The hub may now" on p. 105 before the two
   removal methods on p. 106. That placement is an editorial inference; the
   line remains at its original printed location.

8. pp. 9, 120, 122, 128, 131: the original publication itself revises exhaust
   opening from 50 to 48 degrees before B.D.C., closing from 10 to 8 degrees
   after T.D.C., and nominal tappet clearance from .020 to .019 inch. Older
   values remain in tables, diagrams and prose alongside the printed updates.

9. p. 125: the timing procedure jumps from item 6 to item 8. No missing item 7
   has been reconstructed. p. 128 retains an unexpected reference to p. 86.

10. p. 126: the tappet-setting example mixes R6 and R1. It starts by setting
    R6 clearances while observing R1, then describes R6 valve events and R.1's
    firing point. The intended cylinder sequence has not been established.
    The same page repeats "lower" in "upper and lower / lower inclined shafts."

11. pp. 130-132: the troubleshooting list repeats "Valves" at (v) and (vii)
    and restarts (iii) at "Incorrect jets." Numbering is retained.

12. p. 140, gudgeon-pin fit in rod: the maximum is printed "00125" without
    a decimal point; p. 114 gives .00125 inch. The missing point is retained
    in the reconstructed table.

13. pp. 110, 112, 115 versus pp. 140-141, fits and clearances:
    - Forked-rod end play: .010-.020 inch on p. 115; .008-.020 on p. 140.
    - Camshaft: p. 110 gives rear-bearing end play .001-.003 inch and total
      end play not exceeding .008; p. 140 gives .000-.004, desired minimum
      .002. The scope and differing limits have not been reconciled.
    - Rocker diametrical clearance: .001-.0015 inch on p. 110; .00025-.00175
      on p. 141, desired minimum .001.
    - Oil-pump gear end play: not over .003 inch on p. 112; .002-.007 on
      p. 141, with .003 the desired selection.
    The appendix also separately lists water-pump shaft and bevel-driver
    fits; its values should not be silently substituted into p. 112's prose.

14. Retained apparent printing/worn-letter errors include "interefered"
    (p. 64), "so at to permit of" (p. 65), "pange." after crankshaft (p. 117),
    "Nc. 6" and "bock." (p. 126), "compressicn" (p. 132), "must nct"
    (p. 133), "pump fcr those" (p. 135), "lccking" (p. 136), and "lubricatcr"
    / "attach-attachment" (p. 139). These are documented source readings,
    not new OCR substitutions; see the review notes for minor punctuation.
