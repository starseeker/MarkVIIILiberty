Q. F. HOTCHKISS 2.244-INCH, 6-PDR., 6-CWT. MARK II GUN WITH TANK MOUNTING
Errata and unresolved source readings

Companion to 1919-08-15_Handbook_QF_Hotchkiss_2.244in_6pdr.pdf.
References use printed page/plate numbers. These readings are preserved;
possible corrections have not been established as authoritative.

1. p. 12, spare parts: "2 brushes, sponge" carries an apparent reference E.
   The project flags E as a possible original reference-letter error.

2. p. 12: "2 wrenches, screw driver, for sights (D)" also carries reference E.
   Both the parenthetical D and the separate E are retained; their relationship
   needs editorial comparison rather than an assumed letter substitution.

3. p. 19: "clamp screw brushes" is printed in the source. "Bushes" may have
   been intended, but has not been substituted.

4. Plates II and V: available only in the supplementary, lower-resolution
   scan. Residual bleed-through and limited detail can obscure fine lines or
   labels; no uncertain technical detail has been reconstructed.
