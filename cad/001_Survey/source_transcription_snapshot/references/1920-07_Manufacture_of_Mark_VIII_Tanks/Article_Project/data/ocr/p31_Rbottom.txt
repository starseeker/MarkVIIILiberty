Tests

On February 2, 1919, a sample Mark VIII tank was
received at Rock Island Arsenal from Bridgeport,
Conn. After remaining at this arsenal for a few
days it was ordered out for exhibition purposes to
Clinton, Iowa; Iowa City, Iowa; Ottawa Beach,
Mich., and Denver, Colo. It was then returned and
sent to Savanna Proving Ground, Il., where it re-
ceived a road test of 2,155 miles.
