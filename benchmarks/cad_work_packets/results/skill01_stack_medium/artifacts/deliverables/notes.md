# Synthetic hollow stack implementation

Both scenarios implement `inputs/stack_spec.json` in millimetres using analytic cylindrical/planar BRep geometry. Source identities, dimensions, and both reviewed frames are preserved. All supplied packet input SHA-256 values were verified. These are synthetic interface qualification fixtures, not accepted tank geometry or historical measurements.

Reproduce from the workspace root:

```sh
python3 freecad_python.py deliverables/build.py
python3 freecad_python.py deliverables/check.py
python3 freecad_python.py deliverables/section.py
```

The builder reads both scenarios directly from the supplied JSON and uses the supplied skill's rigid-link world-shape helper. Native files contain identity-frame `Pin`, `Tube`, and `Bush` definitions under hidden `Definitions`. Their cylinders start at local X=0 and extend along +X, retaining the pin bore and both sleeve hollows. `Assembly` carries assembly_frame; its child `Station` carries station_frame. Exactly four direct App::Link occurrences belong to Station, with identity rotations and shared Bush definition. The pin and tube are centered on Station X. BushLeft starts at the tube's left end; BushRight ends at the tube's right end. STEP compounds contain only the four installed solids, after composing Assembly × Station × occurrence placements.

Named OuterRadius, InnerRadius, and Length properties retain source dimensions; SourceKeys map them to the JSON. Geometry is regenerated by the script. Editing these informational properties does not automatically rebuild a solid. Native App::Link occurrences do share their definition geometry.

| Fixture assumption | Nominal | Variant |
|---|---:|---:|
| Pin/bush radial clearance (mm) | 0.1000 | 0.1000 |
| Bush/tube radial clearance (mm) | 0.1500 | 0.1500 |
| Pin projection at each tube end (mm) | 53.975 | 55.975 |
| Axial space between bushes (mm) | 446.1 | 454.1 |

These are geometric fit allowances, not manufacturing tolerances. No manufacturing tolerance bands, material properties, retention method, service loads, historical source drawings, or tank qualification evidence were supplied. None are inferred. No dimensional approximation, chamfer, fillet, press fit, or source modification was introduced.

Verification uses FreeCAD 1.1.1 and OCC 7.8.0. `check.py` separately reopens each native file and STEP, checks hierarchy, exact occurrence identities and sharing, visibility, analytic radii, bounding extents, annular volumes, material/void witnesses, rigid frames using four points, all six overlap pairs, geometric interface distances, STEP solid counts, individual volumes and centroids, and material differences in both directions. Both scenarios pass. Measured overlaps and STEP missing/extra material are zero. Detailed results and runtime versions are in `checks.json`.

Numerical comparison thresholds are 1e-7 mm for lengths/frames and 1e-4 mm³ for volumes. These checking thresholds do not change design dimensions or introduce manufacturing tolerances. The pin and tube share a centroid; STEP matching therefore uses volume as well as centroid.

`sections.png` is a static Station-aligned longitudinal section drawn from exact planar intersections of reopened native BReps at Z=0 by `section.py`. It shows the pin bore, tube wall, two end bushes and overhangs. Raster display resolution is 0.72 mm/pixel, so 0.10/0.15 mm clearances are subpixel; their evidence is numerical. The section is a review aid, not model geometry or a FreeCAD GUI screenshot. Definitions remain hidden after native reopening. No GUI camera styling was required or added. Independent acceptance and source/visual review remain outside this session; passing these checks does not qualify the fixtures for tank use.

The generated section was inspected during this session: both scenarios show the continuous hollow pin, enclosing tube and two separated end bushes. An initial per-pixel solid-classification renderer was stopped because it was slow; the delivered renderer uses exact planar BRep intersections and draws their rectangular section boundaries. This change affects review-image generation only.
